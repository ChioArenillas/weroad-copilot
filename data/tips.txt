### Sri Lanka train travel

The train has 3 classes, and 3rd class is actually the best because all the doors are wide open and the windows open up, so you can get that iconic photo/video leaning out of the train. 2nd class is usually the same, but 1st class has sealed windows that don't open and the doors are usually kept closed (or only one stays open).

During the ride, you can't move between train cars, though conductors often offer to open extra doors for a small tip. Sometimes your group gets split up across different classes—make sure to confirm with your guide that everyone is booked in 3rd or at least 2nd class. In my case, half the group was in 3rd and the other half in 1st, which became a real issue.

As a quick hack: the doors open with a square key. If you insert two standard house keys, for example, you can turn the lock and open it yourself. Some people in my group forced it open and got caught by the conductor, but he just laughed it off—they don't really mind, everyone does it.

### Speedboat at Maldives

Upon arrival at the airport, you're supposedly picked up with a WeRoad sign, but probably they will not have a Weroad sign. Look for someone with a Triton sign (the hotel) instead. If you can't find them, ask any of the people holding signs for any transfer. They all know each other and will tell you who to go with.

SPEEDBOAT TO GO BACK TO AIRPOT AT THE END OF THE TOUR:
For the boat back, ask at Triton and they’ll give you a rate of $25 and take your bags to the port for you. If not, iCom Tours also does it for $25 sometimes. You can book it the day before. They do everything the day before in Maldives, don't stress about it.

### Maafushi tips

BREKFAST TO TAKE AWAY:
Some excursions start at around 6:30 am and you’ll need to order breakfast to go. Triton gives you a pancake, a bun, a sausage, and an egg, but you can ask for a tuna or chicken sandwich and some fruit insted. Make sure you ask for it when you order the take away brekfast.

BABY SHARKS:
In the evening, you can walk to the port. All the way at the end where the bridge is, you can see small sharks, and we even saw a ray.

DJ ON SATURDAYS:
All saturdays there is a DJ on the beach, at bikini beach or aroun that area, ask around. It finishes early, around 11pm.

### SIM card less than 20 euros for both

For the SIM card for both countries, the best option is Dialog. You can buy an eSIM beforehand or right there at the airport normal SIM or esim in Sri Lanka. Buy the package for Sri Lanka, 20GB is enoght and is 4€. 
Then for the Maldives, log in to the Dialog app using your phone number and go to the roaming section. Enter your payment card details and select the roaming package you want (7 days / 5GB for $10 USD). You’ll need to top up 4,000LKR rupees to buy it, which you can easily do while you're still in Sri Lanka whenever you want. 
Once you're in the Maldives, it will activate automatically as long as data roaming is turned on, and your days will start counting from then. It works seamlessly, and in total, it comes out to around €12 for the Maldives package and €4 for Sri Lanka.

If you have any doubts chatgpt will explain step by step 😅

If you do want to exchange do it in the black market and ask for more of the actual exchange

### Climbing in Sri Lanka: What to Keep in Mind

During excursions in Sri Lanka, you’ll often find yourself climbing stairs , lots of them! So I recommend two things: wear sneakers that support your feet or any comfortable shoes for walking, and let your guide know if you have vertigo or a fear of heights, as some stairways can be quite narrow and steep.
If you have extra time, I highly recommend visiting Ambuluwawa Tower, it’s an unforgettable experience!

### Bring euros from Spain

To exchange money in Negombo, there are places where, if you bring euros from Spain, they don’t charge commission like an ATM would. We went to a supermarket next to our hotel and they exchanged it right away. If not, you’ll need to withdraw cash with a physical card.

### Diving in Maafushi

In Maafushi, I recommend going diving or taking a diving course in the morning, as you can see much more marine life.

We went diving in the afternoon and only saw corals and fish, but in the morning they told us there are sharks and manta rays. So if you plan to dive or take a diving course in Maafushi, I recommend going in the morning to see much more marine life.

### ELLA RESTAURANT

We went to a restaurant that also turns into a nightclub called One Love Ella, and the service was terrible. They clearly don’t enjoy having us there, and on top of that, they always try to overcharge. Period. My friends got really upset because they charge extra commission and try to cheat you on the bill, especially if you’re a big group and they think you’re going out to party, so they can slip it in.

I recommend checking out other places in Ella—there are plenty of good restaurants around, but this is definitely not one of them. For the party vibe, the atmosphere is okay, but be very careful when ordering drinks because they will try to mislead you.
